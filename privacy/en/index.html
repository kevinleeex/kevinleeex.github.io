Privacy

Note updated · 2026-11-12

Scope

This is Dengju Li’s personal static website. The new home, resume, privacy and support pages have no accounts, message forms or on-site payment processing. The support page displays locally hosted QR images and links to PayPal; payments are handled by the respective payment services.

Local preferences

Pages read your browser language and system appearance. Manual choices are stored in browser localStorage as resume-language and resume-theme for future language and theme selection. These pages do not send those preferences to the site. Clear local site data for lidengju.com in your browser settings to return to automatic selection.

Hosting and access data

This site is intended to be hosted as static pages by GitHub Pages. The pages themselves load only local scripts, styles, the resume portrait and QR images, with no third-party analytics or fonts. GitHub’s privacy statement describes information its services process, including IP addresses and service usage information; see GitHub’s statement for details.

External links and email

LinkedIn, GitHub, patent, paper and PayPal links take you to other services with their own privacy practices. The support page serves its QR images locally; scanning one in a payment app is handled by that provider. Only clicking the email icon assembles the address locally and hands a draft to your mail app; it does not send a message. Contact details in incoming messages are used to reply. With JavaScript disabled, LinkedIn and GitHub remain available. Splitting the address reduces simple source scanning, not crawlers that execute JavaScript or all spam. Historical pages and PDFs may already expose the address.
